Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password used to create and secure your account. We also collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, in order to power the tracking and reporting features of the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve the Service — this includes authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expense history, and generating summaries and category breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense or account data with third-party services. Where the Service relies on external infrastructure — such as hosting or font delivery — those providers only have access to the minimum technical information required to serve the application, and not to your personal expense records.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
